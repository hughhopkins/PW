Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 March 2026

The short version

PW does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Your passwords are generated locally on your device and never leave it.

Data collection

PW does not collect any personal data. The service name and password you enter are used solely to generate a hash on your device. They are never sent to a server, stored in a database, or shared with any third party.

Data storage

PW does not store your passwords, service names, or any other user input. Everything happens in memory on your device and is discarded when you close the app.

Analytics

The PW website (pwapp.io) uses basic analytics to understand page visits. The iOS and macOS apps do not include any analytics or tracking.

Third-party services

PW does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services. There are no ads, no tracking SDKs, and no external network requests made by the apps.

Your clipboard

When you copy a generated password, it is placed on your device's clipboard. PW does not access or read your clipboard for any other purpose.

Children's privacy

PW does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is ever updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us via Twitter - @pwappio.
